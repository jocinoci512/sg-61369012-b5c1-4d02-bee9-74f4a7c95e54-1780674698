# Crypto Fraud Recovery Platform

## Vision
A premium crypto fraud recovery service that combines financial-grade security expertise with modern fintech accessibility. Serves victims of crypto scams seeking professional blockchain forensics and fund recovery assistance.

## Design
**Direction:** Trust & Authority — established security firm meets modern fintech. Precision, restraint, and subtle luxury signals credibility.

**Evocation:** 1940s bank vault engineering meets contemporary cybersecurity operations center. The tactile weight of embossed letterpress on premium cotton stock, translated to screen through typography hierarchy and purposeful negative space.

**Emotional signature:** Unwavering confidence with understated sophistication.

**Palette:**
- `--primary: 220 90% 22%` (deep navy — authority, security)
- `--primary-foreground: 0 0% 100%` (white text on navy)
- `--accent: 45 65% 58%` (muted gold — premium touch, not ostentatious)
- `--accent-foreground: 220 90% 15%` (dark navy on gold)
- `--background: 40 25% 97%` (warm off-white — approachable)
- `--foreground: 220 30% 15%` (near-black navy — readable)
- `--muted: 220 15% 92%` (light navy-grey)
- `--muted-foreground: 220 20% 40%` (medium slate)
- `--card: 0 0% 100%` (pure white cards)
- `--border: 220 15% 88%` (subtle borders)

**Typography:**
- Headings: Crimson Pro (premium serif, weighted authority)
- Body: Source Sans 3 (clean professional sans, excellent readability)
- Data/code: IBM Plex Mono (technical precision)

**Style direction:** Generous whitespace, asymmetric layouts that guide the eye, gold used sparingly as strategic accent (CTAs, key stats, trust signals), premium card elevation through subtle shadows rather than heavy borders.

## Features
- Scam reporting with 4-step guided form (incident → details → evidence → review)
- 24/7 support via phone/WhatsApp/SMS with one-click copy
- Case studies with recovery outcomes
- Resource center with blockchain security education
- Admin dashboard for case management and analytics
- SEO-optimized blog for crypto fraud awareness
